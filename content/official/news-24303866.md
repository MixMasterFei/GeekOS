 Early Name Reservations for World of Warcraft: Forever are available from October 27 through November 3, giving players an opportunity to secure the names that will become part of their adventures in Azeroth.

 Name Reservation Start Times 

 Europe: October 27 at 18:00 CET

 North America: October 27 at 1:00 p.m. PDT

 Korea: October 28 at 07:00 KST

 Taiwan: October 28 at 07:00 CST

 During the reservation period, eligible players* can create and reserve names for up to three characters total across all Rulesets. This means that if you create two on a PvP Ruleset, you’ll only be able to create one more character in a Normal or Role-Play Ruleset. This restriction only applies to Early Name Reservation.

 Prepare to Launch 

 Make sure your graphics drivers are up to date.

 Download and install the Battle.net desktop app 

 Open the Battle.net desktop app and allow it to update if needed.

 Select World of Warcraft from the list of games.

 In the Game Version menu above the Play button, select World of Warcraft: Forever , then select Install.

 Once installation is complete select Play and choose your Ruleset .

 Create up to 3 characters with your chosen name.

 Choose the names that will carry your heroes into a new age and prepare for your adventures in World of Warcraft: Forever.

 Players who pre-purchase the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack, or limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection will have access to WoW : Forever Early Name Reservation from October 27 through November 3. Character names will be available on a first-come, first-served basis, and desired names cannot be guaranteed.

 For additional information on how character names work, check out the previously published Create a Name of Your Own in WoW: Forever article.

 * Purchase of World of Warcraft: Forever Digital Upgrade or Collector’s Edition required. This benefit only applies to the specific WoW account it was purchased for.
