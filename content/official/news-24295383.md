 Friendship takes center stage at the Trading Post this September as Azeroth celebrates BlizzCon 2026. Ride into your adventures with chameleon-like grace on the Crested Ember Leafmimic mount and visit special Outlet vendors in Dornogal and Silvermoon City featuring discounted and returning items.

 Visit T W** (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar to check out all the latest items*** they have available. Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital cities of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn and in the renewed Silvermoon City where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.

 This Month's Bonus Reward 

 Crested Ember Leafmimic 

Mount

 “ This sturdy leafmimic can be found only in the warmest of areas. Its red scales allow it to hide undiscovered in the heated alcoves it prefers. ” 

 Complete activities to fill the bar at the top of the Traveler’s Log found in the Adventure Guide (Shift-J) *** , and you'll receive this month's bonus reward— the Crested Ember Leafmimic mount.

 Visit the Trading Post Outlet

 To commemorate BlizzCon 2026, visit the Trading Post in Dornogal and in the capital city of Silvermoon City, where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business. You’ll find a cache of treasures from previous stall offerings with the return of many mounts, pets, toys, armor transmogs, and weapon transmogs, as they make their way into the Trading Post once more.

 If you missed them the first time around, you can also purchase previous Monthly Bonus rewards for the first time with Trader’s Tender.
