Azeroth, an untamed frontier begging to be explored. Step into World of Warcraft: Forever and quest through an iconic world reimagined with new and expanded zones, new gameplay enhancements, upgraded graphics, and more.

World of Warcraft: Forever is coming November 4!

WoW: Forever is an invitation to take your first steps in a legendary world of untamed frontiers, warring factions, untold perils, and unseen mysteries. Experience an Azeroth that grows from the roots of WoW’s first age, continually evolving through regular content patches and updates that promise never-ending adventure.

Journey through an updated and expanded Azeroth dense with new stories and secrets hidden across familiar vistas and never-before-seen locales.

Once a secluded island oasis in the sky, Zephras Isle now welcomes the next generation of WoW’s newest race – the Skyborne – to protect this floating island and secure its future.

A sprawling landscape in the Eastern Kingdom which shifts from lush hillsides to ruined keeps of old.

Threats both old and new stalk the slopes of the mountain, eager to claim its power and destroy anyone daring enough to get in their way.

Experience an Azeroth that stays anchored in WoW’s first age while promising never-ending new adventures in an expanding world.

With over 1000+ new quests, new dungeons, and new secrets hidden around all corners, Azeroth promises a journey of discovery and surprise for both novice and veteran adventurers.

Improved lighting and upgraded environments invite you to fall in love with the intimidating expanses and beautiful vistas of Azeroth.

Begin your next adventure as Azeroth’s newest race –the Skyborne – or as one of the newest class and race combos: the Forsaken Paladin or Dwarf Shaman.

Rediscover a favorite class or journey as someone entirely different with revamps to all classes and specializations.

WoW: Forever’s Legacy system rewards account-wide benefits every time you progress a character’s adventure.

Across the new Camping system, updates to Professions, and changes to Honor, each adventurer has new ways to play.

Upgrades to lighting and environments breathe new life into long-beloved locales and yet-to-be-explored lands.

Stand with a new race of windswept elves as their once-secluded home faces an existential threat. Follow the path of the Windshaper Horde or the High Order Alliance in your quest to defend Zephras Isle.

Start your journey into WoW: Forever with new mounts, a new Warcraft III campaign, and more with this limited time package made in celebration of BlizzCon 2026.
